## What did we learn from being grilled?

The question that hurt most challenged why we couldn't simply optimize for 95% classification accuracy like the previous vendor. It exposed that standard ML evaluation completely ignores the asymmetric business penalty between an empty room (~€358 stay value) and a walked guest (€300 penalty plus brand disqualification above 10/1,000 arrivals). We now evaluate all models strictly through the business error bill in euros via `src/contract.py`, calibrating safety buffers on the Choose split to guarantee the walk ceiling before measuring savings.
